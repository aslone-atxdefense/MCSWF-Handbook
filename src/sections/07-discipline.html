ARTICLE 06

Discipline & Academics

How performance is tracked, what accountability looks like day to day, and the three ways a Marine leaves the program.

The four measures

MeasureApplication
AdaptiveInternal measures track performance, attendance, and minor infractions. Evaluations are documented monthly or quarterly. Persistent issues are reviewed by leadership, potentially resulting in dismissal.
IntuitivePolicies proactively adjust to ensure fair opportunities for improvement. Standards remain constant; mission parameters and external factors are continually reassessed.
ResponsiveImmediate action for infractions that compromise mission readiness, viability, or security. Non-compliance is addressed without delay.
DecisiveRecommendations for removal or disciplinary action are made justly and acted upon swiftly.

If you are unaccounted for

Any Marine not accounted for is reported immediately to the SEA, who will attempt contact and continue until each Marine is positively accounted for. Team Leads document the infraction and forward it for your personnel file.

Place of duty is Innovation Tower in downtown Austin unless otherwise specified. Virtual or remote operations may be authorized case-by-case with leadership approval and should not be assumed as an entitlement.

Academic standards

  • Competency. Proficiency is demonstrated through homework, practical exercises, written exams, and performance evaluations. Attendance in all classes and training activities is mandatory.
  • Remediation. Score below 70 in any UT Technical Accelerator course and you undergo remedial training. A designated representative appointed by the SEA determines whether you are fit to graduate or proceed to the operational phase.
  • Evaluation. Marines are evaluated on the “Practice, People, Profession” methodology, distinguishing technical skills, interpersonal abilities, decision-making, and adherence to MCSWF values.

Leave & liberty

Student status

Leave will not be granted while a Marine is in student status — that is, while attending the Technical Accelerator and/or during the 1:1 SME pairing phase.

  • Standard leave. Taken during block leave periods such as winter holidays and 4th of July, and all federal holidays, with MCSWF staff approval.
  • Case-by-case leave. Requests during the Technical Accelerator or 1:1 SME pairing are processed individually.

All leave, liberty, and administrative absences are processed in accordance with MCO 6110.3A.
