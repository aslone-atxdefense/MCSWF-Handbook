ARTICLE 05

Fitness & Medical Readiness

Being a software factory changes nothing about MCO 6100.13A.

Marines assigned to MCSWF must annually complete the Physical Fitness Test (PFT) or Partial PFT, and the Combat Fitness Test (CFT) or Partial CFT.

If you fail a test

Consequences of a single failure

An adverse fitness report · impact to proficiency marks · promotion restriction until the test is passed · a required Page 11 or 6105 counseling entry.

Consecutive failures

Failing consecutive PFTs/PPFTs or CFTs/PCFTs — outside of multiple attempts within the same semi-annual reporting period — means consideration for administrative separation.

Worked example. Fail a PFT and a CFT in the same year, and you are not immediately considered for separation. Fail either test the following year, and you are subject to separation proceedings. Pass a retest after a failure but fail that same test the next year, and you are still considered for separation.

Retesting

You may take multiple tests to improve your score during the current season. Every official test is entered into MCTIMS — higher, lower, or the same as previous attempts. Any PFT/CFT monitored by a properly assigned FFI/CPTR is official.

Individual medical readiness

Marines are expected to remain healthy and prepared for duty at all times, and maintaining medical readiness is every Marine's own responsibility. The SEA monitors readiness and distributes monthly notifications.
