ARTICLE 01

Mission & Values

Why MCSWF exists, and the four values every member is measured against.

Innovation

Embrace and drive continuous improvement in software development methodologies and technologies.

Collaboration

Foster a culture of partnership and open communication across all levels and stakeholders.

Excellence

Strive for the highest standards in software development and delivery, ensuring robust, reliable, and user-centric solutions.

Integrity

Maintain the highest ethical standards in all aspects of operations, earning the trust and confidence of all stakeholders.

Mission

Create a world-class, Marine-led, mission-specific software development capability. Enable Marine modernization efforts by developing applications for commanders at the speed of operations to increase the operating force's lethality and the supporting establishment's efficiency and effectiveness.

Vision

An institutionalized Marine-led software development capability, complete with full career paths in a new occupational field, that revolutionizes how the Marine Corps operates in the digital domain.

MCSWF will become the premier upskilling organization for software development within the Marine Corps and, through partnership with other services and industry, a model for the broader DoD. Through a centralized C2 organization at MCSWF HQ, supported by Data Warfare Platoons at the tactical edge, we will deliver trained Marines capable of producing innovative, mission-focused software solutions — incorporating cutting-edge commercial technology, data science, and AI — that enhance command and control, optimize processes, and accelerate operational efficiencies across all warfighting functions.

The modern battlefield exists over thousands of miles and across multiple domains. MCSWF will address these challenges by supplying commanders with fully trained software Marines, fully enabling them to “locate, close with, and destroy” the enemy.

Endstate

The Marine Corps possesses a scalable organic capability enabling autonomous Marine-led teams that scope and engineer Marine Corps–wide software solutions.

Purpose of this handbook

This handbook gives Marines and staff a one-source document that defines the standards of appearance, conduct, and military customs and courtesy. MCSWF standards are an enforcement and enhancement of Marine Corps standards and orders — they will never violate Marine Corps standards or common expectations for conduct, appearance, and military bearing.

Due to the unique nature of our mission, this is a living document. MCSWF leadership reserves the right to change or adjust it as the organization matures.
