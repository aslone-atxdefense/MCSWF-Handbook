ARTICLE 02

Welcome letter

From the Director and the Senior Enlisted Advisor.

On behalf of the Marine Corps Software Factory, we extend our sincerest congratulations on your selection to the MCSWF. You are one of only 18 active-duty Marines chosen to demonstrate that this capability should be an organic part of our uniformed force. We are counting on you to help carry out our vision of expanding technological education and advancing the development of our Marines.

Becoming a member of the MCSWF is not just an achievement you should be proud of; it is a commitment to uphold the highest standards. Marines in the Software Factory will be held to a higher standard than average, with expectations that exceed those of many in the Fleet Marine Force. You will gain more exposure to the Marine Air-Ground Task Force than most Marines. Through the training we provide and your extensive FMF exposure, your knowledge and experience will reach levels that few Marines will ever attain.

In our experience in the Marine Corps, we have witnessed the shift from analog systems to software-defined solutions. We believe this initiative represents the future of the IT community, regardless of MOS, and could potentially evolve into a new military occupational specialty altogether.

You, the new generation of Marines, are the driving force behind making this vision a permanent and sustainable capability for the Marine Corps.

Again, welcome to the Marine Corps Software Factory. You will find your time here personally challenging and professionally rewarding.

Major
Will Oblak
MCSWF Director
Master Sergeant
Jeff Averette
MCSWF Senior Enlisted Advisor
