ARTICLE 10

Temporary duty (TAD)

Five steps, in order, with hard deadlines. Full detail lives in the TAD CONOPS SOP.

1 Create a Mock DTS Voucher
Before drafting a CONOPS, the requesting Marine creates a Mock DTS Voucher to capture a realistic cost estimate. This is the source data for the CONOPS template. DO NOT submit into DTS before Director approval.
2 Build and Submit the TAD CONOPS
Using the Mock DTS data and inputting the 5 Ws, the requesting Marine completes the MCSWF TAD CONOPS Template and submits it to the Director NLT 30 calendar days before the travel start date.
3 Director Review & Approval
The Director reviews the CONOPS and either approves or disapproves it, normally within 5 business days of receipt. A disapproved CONOPS is returned to the requester with the reason documented so it can be revised and resubmitted.
4 Log to the SharePoint TAD Tracker
Once approved, the requesting Marine logs the trip in the Sharepoint TAD Tracker within 2 business days, attaching or referencing the signed CONOPS.
5 Submit in DTS
With the CONOPS approved and the Tracker updated, the requesting Marine completes the official travel authorization in DTS.
Order matters

Do not submit into DTS before Director approval. The Mock DTS exists to produce the cost estimate for the CONOPS — it is not the authorization.
