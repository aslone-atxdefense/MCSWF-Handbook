Org Wide Battle Rhythms

  • Daily Standup — 0845, Mon-Fri, brief standup to discuss the day's work and any blockers. Attendance is required.
  • Bi-weekly Director Update Briefings — every two weeks in place of Standup, org meets to update Director on team progress, Director briefs organizational updates and strategies.
  • Monthly org offsite — the last Thursday of every month, the whole organization goes offsite for team building.
  • Quarterly family events — every quarter we plan events open to family.
