Course policies

  • Attendance. Regular attendance is crucial. Missing classes or labs may result in being dropped from the course.
  • Late assignments. A two-day grace period for lab assignments, with a 20% penalty.
  • Withdrawal. It is your responsibility to withdraw if necessary, and there may be financial or academic impacts.
  • Incompletes. Granted only for serious extenuating circumstances, and completed by a deadline set with the instructor.
  • Scholastic integrity. Plagiarism or any form of cheating results in a grade of F.

Instructional method

The course splits evenly between lectures and labs — half the class learning theory, half applying it. Coursework is supported via Blackboard, where you access slide decks, assignments, and grades.
